Adaptive Cognitive Training Platform

Adaptive Cognitive Training Platform is a research-inspired application designed to help users improve attention, working memory, planning, and cognitive flexibility through interactive cognitive games. Using gameplay analytics and machine learning, the platform continuously adapts the difficulty of each game based on the user's performance, generates personalized training plans, and provides detailed progress reports through an analytics dashboard, creating an engaging and individualized cognitive training experience.

Rank: 1

Research Hub

A collaborative research management platform that helps teams organize papers, experiments, tasks, meeting notes, and citations in one place. It combines AI-powered paper summarization, semantic search, experiment tracking, and project management to streamline the entire research workflow from literature review to final report. The aim is to reduce the overhead of juggling multiple disconnected tools during a research project and keep a team's work traceable from start to finish.

Rank: 2

Student mess platform

A management system for hostel mess operations, featuring live rush estimation, polls for proposed menu changes, and a notification or pin board for announcements from mess facilitators. The system consists of a student portal, an admin dashboard, an analytics module, a notification system, and a centralized database. Its effectiveness can be evaluated using metrics such as queue waiting time, student satisfaction surveys, and complaint resolution time, while key challenges include collecting sufficient data and encouraging active student participation.

Rank: 3

Low-Pollution Route Notifier

A backend-focused app that notifies users in real time when pollution levels along their pre-defined travel routes are at their lowest. It breaks down pollution scores in 15-minute intervals using the previous week's data and forecasts conditions for upcoming days. The system includes a database for user profiles and routes, a data ingestion layer that fetches AQI, weather and traffic data on a schedule, a pollution score engine, background workers that recompute every 15 minutes, and a notification service across mobile, email and WhatsApp. Advanced features could include low-exposure alternative routes, traffic-adjusted pollution metrics, and specialised paths for joggers and cyclists.

Rank: 4
